Why Become a Mentor

Join Bilunex to shape future generations, develop professional teaching and leadership skills, and guide students beyond grades into lifelong learning, critical thinking, and character development.

Eligibility and Responsibilities

Mentors are responsible for preparing lessons carefully, explaining difficult ideas clearly, maintaining structured lesson frameworks, reviewing students’ weaknesses, and acting as supportive coaches, role models, and professional guides.

Code of Conduct

Mentors commit to professional integrity, treating every student with dignity, avoiding embarrassing students for mistakes, maintaining appropriate boundaries, and adhering to self-evaluation standards.

Recruitment process

What happens after applying?

Applications are reviewed by the Bilunex team, followed by screening and orientation based on mentor self-evaluation guidelines and curriculum standards before onboarding.
