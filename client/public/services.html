02 · Guidance

Mentoring & Guidance

Mentoring can support a student’s personal development and future readiness.

Mentoring is distinct from professional counseling and is not a substitute for psychological care.

Mentorship is driven by trained mentors who act as coaches and role models, selected through rigorous self-evaluation standards to cultivate core life skills, study habits, character development, and independent thinking.

03 · Professional care

Professional Counseling

Professional counseling is provided by a qualified psychologist and is open to everyone, including students, families and adults. It focuses on well-being, stress management and personal challenges, and, for students, mental preparation for national examinations. It is distinct from mentoring and works alongside it where appropriate.

Professional counseling focuses on supporting student well-being, stress management, and mental preparation for national examinations, operating in coordination with family partnerships and mentor support routines.
