Subjects and grade levels

Find the right learning support

Support covers students from Explorers (Grades 1-3) through Achievers (Grades 9-10), spanning Mathematics, English communication, Amharic, Sidama Afoo (Sidaamu Afoo), other core subjects, study habits, communication skills, and character development.

Mentor selection and safeguarding

Information for families

Mentors undergo rigorous self-evaluation and training to serve as professional role models, adhering to parent partnership standards where parents or guardians are encouraged to be present during sessions to protect student well-being.

Mentoring is not a substitute for psychological care. Professional counseling is a separate service provided by a qualified psychologist.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which subjects and grades can Bilunex support?

Bilunex connects learners with mentors who can provide academic support based on their educational needs, subjects, and grade levels. We aim to help students strengthen their understanding, address learning difficulties, and develop effective study habits.

The availability of specific subjects and mentors depends on our current team and service area.

How are mentors selected?

We review mentor applications and assess candidates based on their educational background, subject knowledge, communication skills, motivation, and suitability for working with young learners.

Depending on the recruitment process, selection may include an interview and other relevant assessments. Our goal is to connect families with capable, responsible mentors who understand the importance of supporting students with patience and respect.

What happens after I register my child?

After receiving your registration, the Bilunex team reviews your child's academic needs, preferred subjects, location, schedule, and other relevant requirements.

We then contact you to discuss the next steps and determine whether a suitable mentor is available. We will explain the proposed arrangement before any sessions begin.

Registration does not automatically guarantee immediate mentor placement.

Where does Bilunex provide services?

Bilunex is initially focusing on Hawassa, with limited activity in Addis Ababa. Service availability depends on the location, the availability of suitable mentors, and the type of support requested.

We will communicate the available options before confirming an arrangement.

How much do Bilunex services cost?

Our fees depend on the type of service, learning requirements, schedule, and agreed arrangement.

We discuss the applicable fees with parents before sessions begin so that expectations are clear. Any applicable charges will be communicated in advance.

How does Bilunex help ensure my child's safety?

We recognize that parents are entrusting us with something far more important than academic performance: their child's well-being.

We aim to work with responsible mentors, maintain clear expectations for professional conduct, and encourage open communication with parents.

Session arrangements should be agreed upon with the parent or guardian, including the location, schedule, and appropriate supervision for the learner's age and circumstances.

Parents are encouraged to communicate any concerns to the Bilunex team promptly.

Can parents follow their child's progress?

Yes. We believe parents are important partners in a learner's development.

We aim to maintain communication with parents about learning needs, attendance, progress, and areas that may require additional attention. The frequency and format of progress updates will depend on the service arrangement.

Does Bilunex provide mentoring beyond academic subjects?

Yes. Beyond academic support, Bilunex aims to help learners develop confidence, positive study habits, personal responsibility, and a clearer understanding of their goals.

Mentors can offer appropriate guidance and encouragement based on their experience and the learner's needs. Mentoring does not replace specialized professional services when these are needed.

How can I ask about professional counseling?

Bilunex aims to make professional counseling more accessible by connecting people with appropriately qualified counseling professionals.

If your child or family needs this service, contact the Bilunex team to discuss the concern and available options. Counseling appointments depend on professional availability and the nature of the service requested.

Professional counseling is distinct from the academic guidance and mentoring provided by Bilunex mentors.

What if I have a concern about a mentor or session?

Please contact the Bilunex team directly and explain your concern. We take feedback seriously and aim to address concerns responsibly.

For urgent safety concerns, contact the appropriate local emergency or child-protection services rather than waiting for a response from our team.

How do I register my child?

Select Register Your Student on our website and complete the registration form with accurate information about your child's learning needs, preferred subjects, location, and schedule.

The Bilunex team will review the information and contact you about the next steps.
