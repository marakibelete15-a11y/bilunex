The process

How it works

  1. 01

    Share an enquiry

    Use the separate registration form to tell Bilunex about the student and the support you are exploring.

  2. 02

    Bilunex reviews your request

    Our team reviews the submission within 24 hours to evaluate student developmental needs and alignment with the Bilunex growth framework.

  3. 03

    Discuss possible next steps

    An educational coordinator contacts the family to discuss learning options, mentor matching, and collaborative support strategies.

Subjects and grade levels

Find the right learning support

Support covers students from Explorers (Grades 1-3) through Achievers (Grades 9-10), spanning core subjects, study habits, communication skills, and character development.

Mentor selection and safeguarding

Information for families

Mentors undergo rigorous self-evaluation and training to serve as professional role models, adhering to parent partnership standards where parents or guardians are encouraged to be present during sessions to protect student well-being.

Mentoring is not a substitute for psychological care. Professional counseling is a separate service provided by a qualified psychologist.
