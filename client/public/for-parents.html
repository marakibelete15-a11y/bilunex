Mentor Selection & Safeguarding Framework

At Bilunex, we prioritize safety, integrity, and student emotional wellbeing above all else. Our mentors are carefully selected from reputable universities and colleges based on academic excellence, personal character, and a demonstrated passion for helping younger students thrive.

1. Academic & Disciplinary Vetting:

Mentors demonstrate proven academic achievement in the subjects they teach and maintain strong university academic standing.

2. Supervised Accountability:

Mentors document session progress and student wellbeing through regular Student Support Reports that undergo admin oversight.

3. Safe Communication:

Direct parent transparency is upheld. Mentoring sessions operate under clear behavioral guidelines and child safety standards.
